下载脚本
certutil -urlcache -f -split https://ghps.cc/https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bin456789/reinstall/main/reinstall.bat

执行脚本（管理员权限./）
reinstall.bat windows --iso='11111111' --image-name='xxxxxxxx'

win11一键脚本示例
reinstall.bat windows --iso='https://drive.massgrave.dev/zh-cn_windows_11_business_editions_version_23h2_x64_dvd_2a79e0f1.iso' --image-name='Windows 11 Pro'

iso镜像下载地址：在https://massgrave.dev/genuine-installation-media.html网站查找，将下载链接粘贴到上方11111111处。

image-name：获取方式在Bin大佬github的README中有详细说明：https://github.com/bin456789/reinstall#readme
