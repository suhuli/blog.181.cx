<p>下载脚本<br/>
certutil -urlcache -f -split https://ghps.cc/https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bin456789/reinstall/main/reinstall.bat</p>
<p>执行脚本（管理员权限./）<br/>
reinstall.bat windows --iso='11111111'  --image-name='xxxxxxxx'</p>
<p>win11一键脚本示例<br/>
reinstall.bat windows --iso='https://drive.massgrave.dev/zh-cn_windows_11_business_editions_version_23h2_x64_dvd_2a79e0f1.iso'  --image-name='Windows 11 Pro'</p>
<p>iso镜像下载地址：在https://massgrave.dev/genuine-installation-media.html网站查找，将下载链接粘贴到上方11111111处。</p>
<p>image-name：获取方式在Bin大佬github的README中有详细说明：https://github.com/bin456789/reinstall#readme</p>
